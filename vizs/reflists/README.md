# Analyses and visualizations involving the truelists/falselists for experiment 1

- `list-agg.ipynb` produces graphs that relate the proportion of falselisted items to the attribution method and to the $p$ and $m$ parameters. For some of the analyses, it relies on word frequency lists, which are compiled with `compute_freq_index.ipynb` and stored in `freqs`.
- `reflist-quality.ipynb` assesses the quality of the truelists and falselists by comparing them with the manual annotations done independently.
